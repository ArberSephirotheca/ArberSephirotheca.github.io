Hi, I'm a 2nd-year PhD student in Computer Science at [UC Santa Cruz](https://www.ucsc.edu/about/). I'm advised by [Prof. Tyler Sorensen](https://users.soe.ucsc.edu/~tsorensen/) and a member of the [LSD Lab](https://lsd.ucsc.edu/). My research focuses on GPGPU systems, heterogeneous programming, and compiler infrastructure.

I am currently modeling GPU forward progress guarantees and concurrency semantics for SPIR-V using [TLA+](https://lamport.azurewebsites.net/tla/tla.html). You can also browse my [publications](@/publications/_index.md), download my [CV](/files/Zheyuan_Chen_CV.pdf), or find project code on [GitHub](https://github.com/ArberSephirotheca).
