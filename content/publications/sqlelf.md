`sqlelf` explores a SQL-centric workflow for ELF analysis by modeling ELF objects as relational data. That framing makes it possible to inspect low-level binary structure with familiar query tools rather than ad hoc scripts.

The preprint is available on arXiv, and the site also hosts a PDF copy for convenience.
